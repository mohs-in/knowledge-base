If you look at the above attached resource,

Here you have `cid`, `cname`, `caddr`, `czip`, and `DOB` of the customer. But you don't have the age in the database.

But Let's say you want to restrict some of the products in Amazon to some users based on age. Then you can derive age using DOB.

In ER-Diagram we represent these with dotted ellipse.

> [!NOTE] Note
> Derived attributes are not typically stored in the database, physically. Instead it can be derived using an algorithm.
