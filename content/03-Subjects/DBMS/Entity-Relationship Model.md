The purpose of ER-Diagram or ER-Model  is to get the high level or logical view of the database.

E - [[Entity]] | R - Relationship

In the Real-World various teams work together to use the databases. For everyone to have a good understanding of what we have in the database is very crucial.

So, ER-Model or ER-Diagram gives us that high level picture.
